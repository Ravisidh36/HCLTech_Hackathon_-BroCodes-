## 1. Purpose

This policy sets visiting arrangements for patients admitted to the Intensive Care Unit (ICU).

## 2. Visiting Hours

### 2.1 Standard Hours

ICU visiting is permitted from 12:00 to 13:00 and from 18:00 to 20:00 daily.

### 2.2 Number of Visitors

A maximum of two visitors are allowed at the bedside at any time.

### 2.3 Children

Children under 12 years are not permitted in the ICU.

## 3. Infection Control

All visitors must sanitise their hands and wear a mask inside the ICU.
