# General Ward Visiting Policy

Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all general (non-ICU) inpatient wards.

## 2. Visiting Hours

### 2.1 Standard Hours

General ward visiting is permitted from 16:00 to 20:00 daily.

### 2.2 Number of Visitors

Up to three visitors may be present at the bedside at one time.

## 3. Visitor Passes

Each admitted patient receives two visitor passes at admission. Lost passes are replaced at the
main reception.
