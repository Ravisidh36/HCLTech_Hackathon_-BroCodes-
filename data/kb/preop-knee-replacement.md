# Pre-operative Protocol for Total Knee Replacement

Department of Orthopaedics, Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Scope

This protocol applies to all adult patients scheduled for elective total knee replacement.

## 2. Required Pre-operative Tests

All of the following tests must be completed, with results valid on the day of surgery, before
the patient is cleared for surgery.

| Test | Code | Result valid for |
|---|---|---|
| Haemoglobin | HGB | 30 days |
| Serum Creatinine | CREAT | 30 days |
| Prothrombin Time (INR) | PTINR | 30 days |
| Electrocardiogram | ECG | 30 days |
| HbA1c (glycated haemoglobin) | HBA1C | 90 days |

## 3. Readiness Checklist

### 3.1 Clearance Conditions

A patient is ready for surgery only when all of these are true:

1. Every test in section 2 is complete and within its validity period.
2. Insurance pre-authorisation is approved, or the patient has accepted self-payment.
3. The operating surgeon has a confirmed theatre slot on the planned date.
4. Signed informed consent is on file.

### 3.2 Pending Items

If any condition in 3.1 is not met, surgery must not proceed until the item is resolved.

## 4. Day of Surgery

Patients must fast for 8 hours before surgery and arrive at the surgical admissions unit by 06:30.
