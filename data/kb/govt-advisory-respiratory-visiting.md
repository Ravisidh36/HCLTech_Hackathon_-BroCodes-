# State Health Advisory: Hospital Visiting During Respiratory Infection Season

Synthetic regulatory advisory for demonstration only. Issued by the (fictional) State Directorate
of Health Services. Applies to all hospitals in the state and overrides conflicting hospital policies.

## 1. Applicability

This advisory applies to general inpatient wards from 1 September 2026 until withdrawn.

## 2. Visiting Restrictions

### 2.1 Visiting Hours

General ward visiting is restricted to 17:00 to 19:00 daily.

### 2.2 Number of Visitors

Only one visitor per patient is allowed at a time.

### 2.3 Masks

Visitors must wear a surgical mask throughout the visit.

## 3. Compliance

Hospitals must display this advisory at all ward entrances.
