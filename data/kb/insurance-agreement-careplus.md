# CarePlus Insurance Agreement Summary

Agreement between Demo City Hospital and Demo Health Insurance Ltd. Synthetic document for
demonstration only. Individual eligibility is always confirmed by the hospital's insurance
verification system, not by this summary.

## 1. Plans Covered

The hospital accepts the CarePlus Gold and CarePlus Basic plans for cashless treatment.

## 2. Procedure Coverage

| Procedure | CarePlus Gold | CarePlus Basic |
|---|---|---|
| Knee Replacement | Covered, 10% co-pay, pre-authorisation required, 365-day waiting period | Not covered |
| MRI Scan | Covered, no co-pay | Covered, 20% co-pay, pre-authorisation required, 30-day waiting period |
| Cardiology Consultation | Covered, no co-pay | Covered, 10% co-pay |
| Cataract Surgery | Covered, 15% co-pay, pre-authorisation required, 730-day waiting period | Covered, 25% co-pay, pre-authorisation required, 730-day waiting period |

## 3. Pre-authorisation

### 3.1 Process

The billing desk submits pre-authorisation requests to the insurer. Decisions are usually received
within 48 hours.

### 3.2 Validity

An approved pre-authorisation is valid for 60 days from approval.
