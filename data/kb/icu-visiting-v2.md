# ICU Visiting Policy

Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only. Supersedes version 1.0.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets visiting arrangements for patients admitted to the Intensive Care Unit (ICU),
balancing family support with infection control and patient rest.

## 2. Visiting Hours

### 2.1 Standard Hours

ICU visiting is permitted from 10:00 to 11:00 and from 16:00 to 18:00 daily.

### 2.2 Number of Visitors

A maximum of two visitors are allowed at the bedside at any time.

### 2.3 Children

Children under 12 years are not permitted in the ICU. The ICU charge nurse may approve exceptions.

## 3. Infection Control

### 3.1 Hand Hygiene

All visitors must sanitise their hands when entering and leaving the ICU.

### 3.2 Illness

Visitors with fever, cough or other symptoms of infection must not visit.

## 4. Exceptions

Visiting outside standard hours, including for end-of-life care, requires approval from the
ICU consultant on duty.
