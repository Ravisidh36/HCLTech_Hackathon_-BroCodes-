# Inpatient Admission Policy

Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Planned Admissions

### 1.1 Documents Required

Patients must bring a government photo ID, the admission advice from their doctor, their insurance
card and a list of current medicines.

### 1.2 Admission Desk

The admission desk on the ground floor is open from 07:00 to 21:00. Planned admissions should
report at least two hours before the scheduled procedure.

## 2. Emergency Admissions

Emergency admissions are processed by the Emergency Department at any time. Documents may be
submitted within 24 hours of admission.

## 3. Deposits

Insured patients with approved pre-authorisation are not required to pay an admission deposit.
Other patients pay a deposit as advised by the billing desk.
