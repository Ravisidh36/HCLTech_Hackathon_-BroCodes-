# MRI Scan Scheduling Procedure

Department of Radiology, Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Who Can Request an MRI

An MRI scan can be scheduled only with a written referral from a hospital or registered doctor.

## 2. Steps to Schedule an MRI

### 2.1 Booking

1. Bring or upload the doctor's referral to the Radiology reception or the patient portal.
2. Radiology staff review the referral and confirm the scan type within one working day.
3. Choose an available slot offered by Radiology reception or through the patient portal.
4. Complete the MRI safety questionnaire before the appointment.

### 2.2 Insurance

If the patient's insurance plan requires pre-authorisation for MRI, the billing desk submits the
request after the referral is confirmed. The scan is booked once pre-authorisation is approved.

## 3. Preparation

### 3.1 Before the Scan

Remove all metal objects. Inform staff about pacemakers, implants or pregnancy.

### 3.2 Arrival

Arrive 30 minutes before the appointment time for registration and safety screening.
