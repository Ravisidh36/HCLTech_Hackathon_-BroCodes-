# ICU Visiting Policy

Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets visiting arrangements for patients admitted to the Intensive Care Unit (ICU).

## 2. Visiting Hours

### 2.1 Standard Hours

ICU visiting is permitted from 11:00 to 12:00 and from 17:00 to 18:00 daily.

### 2.2 Number of Visitors

A maximum of one visitor is allowed at the bedside at any time.

## 3. Visitor Conduct

Visitors must sign in at the ICU reception desk and follow instructions from nursing staff.
