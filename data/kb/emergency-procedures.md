# Emergency Department Procedures for Patients and Visitors

Emergency Department, Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Access

The Emergency Department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The entrance is on the east side
of the main building.

## 2. Triage

### 2.1 Assessment

Every patient is assessed by a triage nurse on arrival. Patients are seen in order of clinical
urgency, not arrival time.

### 2.2 Life-threatening Emergencies

Patients with chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding or loss of consciousness are
treated immediately.

## 3. Emergency Contact

For an ambulance, call the hospital emergency line 1066 (fictional number).
