# Patient Discharge Guidelines

Demo City Hospital. Synthetic document for demonstration only.

## 1. Discharge Timing

Discharges are processed between 10:00 and 13:00 on the day the treating doctor approves discharge.

## 2. Discharge Summary

### 2.1 Contents

Every patient receives a written discharge summary with the diagnosis, procedures performed,
medicines to continue, follow-up appointment and warning signs that need urgent attention.

### 2.2 Questions

Questions about the discharge summary should be directed to the ward nurse before leaving or to
the treating doctor at the follow-up appointment.

## 3. Billing Clearance

Final bills are settled at the billing desk. For insured patients, the billing desk coordinates
the final claim with the insurer.

## 4. After Discharge

Patients should attend the follow-up appointment listed in the discharge summary. Any new or
worsening symptoms should be reported to the hospital or the Emergency Department.
